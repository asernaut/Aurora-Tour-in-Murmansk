Main Highlights

  • Scenic Tundra Drive: 130 km road through endless Arctic landscapes and wind farms.
  • Ship Graveyard: Iconic wooden fishing boats resting on the Barents Sea shore.
  • Dragon Eggs Beach: Unique coastline covered in giant round boulders polished by the Arctic Ocean.
  • Batareysky Waterfall: Dramatic coastal waterfall flowing directly toward the sea (frozen into ice sculptures in winter).
  • Arctic Swing & Giant Throne: Famous photo spots right on the sandy beach.

Good to Know Before You Go

  • Weather & Wind: Teriberka sits directly on the open Barents Sea, so it is noticeably windier than Murmansk. Bring a windproof jacket, warm hat, and waterproof winter boots.
  • Winter Road Conditions: During heavy blizzards, the road to Teriberka can occasionally close temporarily for safety. We always monitor road updates and adjust the schedule if needed.
