What to Expect

  • Dog Sledding Ride: Experience the power and excitement of a real husky team pulling your sled along scenic snowy forest tracks.
  • Husky Interaction & Photos: Spend plenty of time petting, hugging, and taking photos with super-friendly Siberian and Alaskan huskies.
  • Arctic Kennel Stories: Learn from local mushers how Arctic sled dogs are trained, fed, and cared for in polar conditions.
  • Warm-up Break: Relax with hot tea in a cozy wooden hut after your ride.

What’s Included

  • Hotel pick-up and drop-off in Murmansk
  • Entrance to the Husky Park
  • Guided dog sledding ride (subject to snow cover)
  • Free time for husky cuddles and photography
  • Hot tea to warm up after the outdoor activity

Good to Know

  • Seasonal availability: Dog sledding runs when there is sufficient snow cover (usually December to April). Outside the snow season, husky trekking and park visits are available.
  • Clothing: Dress warmly in windproof winter layers, insulated boots, and mittens — riding on the sled feels breezy.
  • Great for everyone: Safe and exciting for solo travelers, couples, and families with children.
